In my ninth week, I shifted my focus from coding to finding relevant research for our project. Here’s a summary of what I
accomplished:

- **Literature Review:** I began conducting a literature review on AI in education, focusing on challenges, solutions, and
what has and has not worked when integrating AI into educational settings. I also searched for qualitative and
quantitative studies that used Reddit data to study education-related discussions. To organize the findings, I created a
spreadsheet of relevant papers, including their titles, links, bullet-point summaries, and differences from our paper,
ranking them by relevance.
- **Team Meeting:** All three of us met on Zoom to discuss the papers I found during my literature review. We identified
one paper as the most relevant to our research and decided to focus on it, using its introduction and related works
section to discover additional relevant literature.

I’m looking forward to continuing the literature review and using these papers to strengthen the foundation of our
research. Stay tuned for more updates!
