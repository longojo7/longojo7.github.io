In my eighth week, our team was able to finish the initial phase of coding. Here’s a summary of what I accomplished:

- **Recoding:** In the early part of the week, I went back and recoded the 10 initial sample files using our finalized
codebook. Our goal was to see how closely each team member coded those files to better understand each other's thought
processes before we moved on to new files.
- **Team Collaboration Meeting:** All three of us met on Zoom to review how we coded the files and compare them with how
they coded theirs. This involved reviewing each code and subcode and the corresponding text segments to identify any
overlap or discrepancies.
- **Data Collection:** I used the randomizer program to collect 5 posts from each subreddit in a JSONL file. After that, I
ran the text-file generation program to convert the 10 posts into text files, so they are ready for coding in MAXQDA.

Next week, we will finish reviewing how each of us coded the 10 files so we are ready to move on to the next 10. Stay
tuned for more updates!
