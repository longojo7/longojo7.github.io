This was the final week of the DREAM program. Early in the week, I finished writing a rough draft of the results section based on Sakib's 
takeaways about what should be in the poster. Then, on Wednesday, Madiha edited and finalized the introduction, background, and 
methodology section. We created a LaTeX file on Overleaf using the ACM template and added those parts, including citations. Then 
Sakib edited and finalized the results, discussion, and limitations section and added it to our LaTeX file. It wouldn't be research 
if we hadn't missed one thing near the deadline: I hadn't registered for my ORCID ID. Once we put all our sections into the poster
template, I did one more pass to fix clarity issues and typos, and then we submitted our poster to SIGCSE TS 2027!

On Friday, our DREAM cohort got together for one final meeting to recap our journey, what we learned, and our final product, 
and to share any comments or suggestions for improvement. We also talked about next year's program, our relationships with our 
advisors and mentors, and whether we wanted to pursue a PhD after DREAM, before ending with final goodbyes and thank-yous. This
has been one of the most incredible experiences I've had, and I really want to thank all the faculty involved with DREAM for 
developing this program and giving us the chance to try research. I also want to thank my mentor and research advisor, Sakib, 
and my teammate and advisor, Madiha, for letting me do research with them and teaching me all about HCI research.
